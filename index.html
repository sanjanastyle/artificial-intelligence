🧠 AI Basics

Understanding Artificial Intelligence

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence, commonly known as AI, is a field of computer science that focuses on creating computer systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence.

AI systems can work with information, recognize patterns, understand language, generate content and support decision-making.

🧠

Learning

AI systems can learn patterns from data.

👁️

Recognition

AI can process images, sounds and other information.

💬

Language

AI can process and generate human language.

⚙️

Automation

AI can assist with repetitive and complex tasks.

03

📜 History of AI

From early ideas to modern AI

1950

Alan Turing

Alan Turing published influential work about machine intelligence and introduced the idea behind the Turing Test.

1956

Dartmouth Conference

The Dartmouth research project helped establish Artificial Intelligence as a field of study.

1997

Deep Blue

IBM's Deep Blue defeated world chess champion Garry Kasparov.

2010s

Deep Learning

Deep learning became increasingly important in areas such as image and speech recognition.

Today

Modern AI

AI is now used in education, healthcare, business, entertainment and many other areas.

🧩 Types of AI

Different concepts of Artificial Intelligence

🎯

Narrow AI

AI designed to perform specific tasks. Most AI systems used today fall into this category.

🧠

General AI

A theoretical concept describing AI capable of performing a broad range of intellectual tasks.

🚀

Super AI

A hypothetical concept referring to AI that could exceed human capabilities across many areas.

06

📊 Machine Learning

Learning patterns from data

What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning is a part of AI that allows computer systems to learn patterns from data and use those patterns to make predictions or perform tasks.

📚

Supervised Learning

Learns from examples where the desired answers are known.

🔍

Unsupervised Learning

Finds patterns or groups in data without labeled answers.

🎮

Reinforcement Learning

Learns through feedback from actions and results.

07

💬 Natural Language Processing

AI and human language

What is NLP?

Natural Language Processing, or NLP, is an area of AI that helps computers process and work with human language.

🗣️

Speech Recognition

Converts spoken language into text or commands.

🌐

Translation

Helps computers translate between languages.

✍️

Text Analysis

AI can analyze and organize written information.

🤖

Chatbots

NLP helps chatbots communicate using human language.

08

👁️ Computer Vision

AI and visual information

What is Computer Vision?

Computer Vision is an area of AI that helps computers analyze and interpret visual information such as images and video.

📸

Image Recognition

Identifying objects or patterns in images.

🎥

Video Analysis

Analyzing information from video frames.

🚗

Driver Assistance

Visual systems can help identify objects around vehicles.

⚠️ Risks & Challenges

Understanding AI limitations

🔒

Privacy

AI systems may process large amounts of personal information.

⚖️

Bias

AI systems can produce unfair results when data or design contains bias.

❌

Incorrect Information
